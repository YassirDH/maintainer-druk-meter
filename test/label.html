How do you judge a comment?
  • Negative: frustration, complaining, applying pressure ("when will this finally be fixed?"), anger, disappointment, rudeness.
  • Neutral: technical or factual, a question without emotion, a status update.
  • Positive: thanks, praise, enthusiasm, appreciation.
Main rule: judge the tone of the writer, not the topic. "Fatal error on startup, here is the log" is about something bad, but it is written neutrally. In doubt, or mixed? Pick what dominates and write it in the note.
