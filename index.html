Read this as a signal, not a verdict.
  • The model was trained on tweets, not on programmer language. Technical words such as kill, fatal or failure can wrongly make a matter-of-fact comment negative. See the test report for how often it is right.
  • Only public, English GitHub comments. People who email, chat or stay silent are not counted. Small projects (xz, zlib) are almost silent, so the meter sees most where most people are already looking.
  • Periods with fewer than 10 comments are shown as hollow dots: their percentage is unreliable.
